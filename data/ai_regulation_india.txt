Title: AI Regulation in India (High-level Notes)

India is working on AI governance through frameworks, advisory documents, and broader digital regulation efforts.
Key themes include:
1) Responsible AI: fairness, transparency, accountability, and explainability.
2) Data privacy: how AI systems collect, process, and store user data.
3) Risk-based governance: stricter rules for high-risk systems (healthcare, finance, public services).
4) Deepfakes and misinformation: proposals often discuss labeling, detection, and penalties.
5) Public sector adoption: AI in healthcare, agriculture, and education needs safety and reliability.

Common challenges:
- Fast tech growth vs slow legal changes
- Bias and discrimination risks
- Lack of high-quality public datasets
- Compute cost and infrastructure gaps
- Skill gap in AI governance

Conclusion:
India’s AI regulation is evolving, focusing on safety + innovation balance.
