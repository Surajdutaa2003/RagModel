Title: FAQs

Q: What is RAG?
A: Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It retrieves relevant chunks from documents and then generates an answer.

Q: Why embeddings?
A: Embeddings convert text into vectors so we can search semantically.

Q: What is a vector database?
A: A storage system for embeddings that supports similarity search.

Q: What is chunking?
A: Splitting text into smaller pieces to store and retrieve efficiently.
